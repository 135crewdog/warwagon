Credits

        

          
Flight model
Built from public KC-135 research: NASA’s wind-tunnel reports on the KC-135A (TM 73,250 and the winglet programme), the Air Force’s AFFDL-TR-77-23 for the Mach effects, NASA’s in-flight drag measurements, and Crenshaw’s AFIT thesis for the inertias, dampings and flight-test modes. Every number’s source is in the repository’s data file. The engines are the F108’s certified ratings from EASA’s type certificate, lapsed by the equations in the OpenAP paper (CC BY).

          
Camera and HUD
After Ryan Vazquez’s flight-simulator series and its code, MIT licence.

          
The jet
A placeholder built in code from the fact sheet’s dimensions, until a KC-135 model replaces it.

          
3D
three.js, MIT licence.

135crewdog, with Claude Code.

        

      

    


    

      

        
Rotate your phone

        
War Wagon is flown in landscape. If it won't rotate, turn off Portrait Orientation Lock in Control Center.

      

    


    

      

        
Motion access denied

        
War Wagon flies by tilting the phone, so it needs motion access. To fix it: close the app completely, open it again, tap Fly and choose Allow.
